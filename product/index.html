The batch layer between recording and publishing.

BlinkClip is for the jobs that happen after recording and before publishing: repeated trims, crops, intros, conversions, and exports that should not require a timeline.

The creator bottleneck

Stop opening a timeline for repeat work.

You do not need a full editor when a folder of clips needs the same trim, crop, intro, or export. Pick the action once and send the batch out with consistent settings.

The old way

Manual Timeline

  • Open Premiere or Resolve for every file
  • Wait for the workspace to load assets
  • Manually scrub and cut the head or tail
  • Export one by one through a media encoder
The BlinkClip way

Batch Precision

  • Drop in a folder of clips
  • Choose the trim, crop, convert, or Add Intro action once
  • Process unlimited clips with the same settings
  • Export locally on your Mac

How it works

Three steps from messy folder to ready-to-upload files.

1

Select clips

Drop in the folder of videos waiting for the same cleanup pass.

2

Choose action

3

Export

Send the batch out with consistent settings and keep moving.

Built for batches

No fixed clip limit, no separate FFmpeg setup, no checkout wall.

What BlinkClip is best at

Drop in an unlimited clip batch, choose the repeated operation, and export consistent files from your Mac. Your Mac and source media decide the practical batch size.

Where it fits

Use BlinkClip before your creative editor, after a recording session, before upload, or when a client asks for the same change across many deliverables.
