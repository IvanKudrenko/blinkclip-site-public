Notices

Legal and product status notes

Early-access software

BlinkClip is currently free early-access macOS software. Test important jobs with small batches first, and keep original videos until you have verified exported files.

Local processing

BlinkClip is built around local macOS video processing. Core batch processing happens on your Mac.

Support messages

When you email blinkclip.app@gmail.com, your message and any context you include are used to answer the request and improve BlinkClip.

Open-source notices

BlinkClip includes third-party open-source components for media processing and app updates. Notices are provided in the app and may change between public builds.

No paid checkout yet

This site is focused on free early access downloads. Pro features may be planned later, but paid billing is not presented as active here.
