A look at BlinkClip for Android

Why BlinkClip?

Some video tasks don’t need a timeline. Trim a clip, crop the frame, convert a file, merge videos, or add media with a focused action.

Apply repeated tasks to multiple videos without setting up a traditional editing project. Processing happens locally on your Android device, so your videos stay with you.

How testing works

Use the same Google Account for each step.

Google Play requires at least 12 opted-in testers for 14 consecutive days before we can apply for production access.

  1. Join the Google Group

    Join the BlinkClip Android beta group (blinkclip-android-beta@googlegroups.com) with the Google Account you use for Google Play. Group membership makes you eligible; it does not opt you into the test.

    Join the Google Group

  2. Opt in through Google Play

    After joining the group, open the official Google Play testing link with the same account and choose to become a tester.

    Opt in through Google Play

  3. Install BlinkClip

    Once you’ve opted in, install BlinkClip on your Android phone from its Google Play listing.

    Install BlinkClip from Google Play

  4. Test and share feedback

    Try real video tasks, check your exports, and send feedback below. Stay opted in through Google Play for at least 14 consecutive days.

What to test

Start with a small video task you would normally do on your phone. Check the exported result, too.

  • Convert and export a video.
  • Use Quick Crop with touch gestures.
  • Merge or process multiple videos.
  • Use Overlay to add an image and verify the result.

Feel free to explore the other features as well. Keep your original files until you’ve checked your exports.

Tell us how it went

Bug reports, confusing interactions, and device-specific issues are all welcome. Tell us what you tried, what happened, and what you expected. Your phone model and Android version can help us understand the issue.

A few practical answers

Is it free?

Yes. Joining the Android beta and using the test app is free.

Do I need an Android phone?

Yes. You need an Android phone with Google Play and an eligible Google Account. Use the same account for the group and Google Play.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. Video processing happens locally on your Android device.

Why 14 days?

Google Play requires at least 12 testers opted in continuously for 14 consecutive days before we can apply for production access. Joining the group or submitting the form does not start this period; opting in through Google Play does.

Google Play’s testing requirements

Is BlinkClip for Android publicly released?

Not yet. This is a closed Android beta. Group membership makes you eligible, but you must also opt in through Google Play before installing.
