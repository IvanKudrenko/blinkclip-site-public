Privacy Policy

Your media stays under your control

This policy explains how BlinkClip handles data across Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac, BlinkClip Online, and this website. The native apps and browser editor process media locally; website measurement and Mac update checks are the principal network data flows.

1. Overview and products covered

This Privacy Policy covers the BlinkClip Android app, the BlinkClip app for iPhone and iPad (including its Share Extension), BlinkClip for macOS, BlinkClip Online, and the BlinkClip website at blinkclip.baproj.com. It does not describe the independent practices of stores, file providers, browsers, email providers, or other services you choose to use with BlinkClip.

2. Who operates BlinkClip and how to contact us

BlinkClip is operated by the developer identified on this website as BA / BlinkClip. For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, email blinkclip.app@gmail.com. BlinkClip does not require an account, and the reviewed products do not collect a name or email address unless you choose to send one in a support message or an Android beta signup or feedback submission.

3. What BlinkClip accesses and how media is processed

When you select a video, audio file, image, folder, or Photos item, BlinkClip accesses that item and technical information needed to perform the requested operation, such as its filename, file size, duration, dimensions, tracks, codecs, and frame rate. BlinkClip uses this information to preview, validate, process, name, and export your results.

Imported source files are not sold or used to train an artificial-intelligence model. The reviewed native apps and BlinkClip Online do not send media content, filenames, media metadata, or processing diagnostics to BlinkClip analytics. An export remains under your control in the location, Photos library, media collection, or share target you select.

4. Android

  • BlinkClip uses Android's system Photo Picker, document access, and incoming share intents so you choose the media it can read. It does not request broad modern photo or video-library permission.
  • Processing uses Android Media3 on the device. A foreground media-processing service and notification keep a user-started job visible while it runs.
  • Working files are created in BlinkClip's private cache and are deleted after processing, cancellation, failure, or later cleanup. Thumbnails are held in an in-memory cache.
  • Finished video and audio files are published through Android MediaStore to Movies/BlinkClip or Music/BlinkClip. They remain there until you delete them, even if you clear BlinkClip's app data.
  • The reviewed production dependencies are AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, and Media3. The manifest contains no Internet permission, and the source contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, account, or identifier SDK.
  • Release logs contain concise error categories. More detailed diagnostics, which can include local technical context, are limited to debug builds and are not uploaded by the app.

5. iPhone and iPad

  • The system Photos picker and Files picker provide only the items you choose. BlinkClip copies selected media into its app-support container so it can process the files reliably without ongoing provider access.
  • The Share Extension copies videos you explicitly share into a BlinkClip app-group inbox. The main app moves completed batches into its private workspace and removes the inbox batch. Incomplete extension batches are eligible for local cleanup after 24 hours.
  • Imported media, auxiliary files, workflow settings, session state, and finished results can remain in BlinkClip's local app container between launches. Removing an imported item or clearing a result removes its unreferenced BlinkClip-owned copy; deleting the app removes its app container, subject to Apple's platform behavior.
  • Saving to Photos, exporting to Files, or sharing a result occurs only when you choose that action. The destination service then handles the copy under its own terms.
  • The reviewed app and extension use Apple system frameworks and contain no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, account, or network client. Media processing occurs on the device.

6. macOS

  • BlinkClip reads the files and folders you select and writes finished files to the output location you choose. Source files are not uploaded for processing.
  • FFmpeg and Apple media frameworks run locally. Temporary files created for multi-step jobs are removed after the operation on a best-effort basis. Exported files remain until you delete them.
  • Preferences such as output paths, processing settings, saved workflows, and plan state are stored locally in macOS preferences. Local console or system logs can include filenames, paths, commands, timing, and error details; BlinkClip does not upload those logs automatically.
  • The Sparkle updater requests BlinkClip's GitHub-hosted update feed and release files to check for and install updates. GitHub and its delivery providers receive the ordinary connection information associated with those requests.
  • No advertising, product-analytics, or crash-reporting SDK was found in the reviewed Mac app source.

7. BlinkClip Online

BlinkClip Online downloads the BlinkClip interface and self-hosted FFmpeg WebAssembly runtime, then processes selected media inside the browser tab. The processing engine does not upload your source files, auxiliary media, filenames, metadata, results, or FFmpeg diagnostics to a remote processing service.

Source and working files are written to the in-tab FFmpeg virtual file system and are removed after each pipeline on a best-effort basis. Result blobs remain in browser memory until you reset the workspace, replace the result, or close the tab. Files you download or save remain wherever you chose to store them. Browser extensions, managed devices, and browser or operating-system features are outside BlinkClip's control.

8. Website analytics, cookies, and local storage

The website uses Google Analytics 4 with Advanced Consent Mode. Analytics storage, advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization are denied by default. Before you choose, or after you disable analytics, Google still receives cookieless measurement pings that can include consent state, page and event information, timestamp, user agent, referrer, and network information such as IP address. Google does not read or write first-party Analytics cookies in that state. Advertising features, Google Signals, and ad personalization are disabled.

If you allow analytics, GA4 may use Analytics cookies and BlinkClip starts Amplitude in its EU data zone. These services can receive a pseudonymous browser identifier, page views, sessions, referral domain, campaign values, approximate country or region derived from IP address, download events, and limited BlinkClip Online events such as files added, actions added, or processing success or failure. Event counts do not include media content, filenames, email addresses, or exact location. Amplitude is configured without session replay, form capture, broad element capture, network capture, performance tracking, or remote diagnostics.

BlinkClip stores your language and analytics choice in local storage. When analytics is allowed, a sanitized landing path, referrer domain, and UTM campaign values can be kept in session storage, and Amplitude browser identifiers are configured for up to 365 days. GA4 and Amplitude records are also subject to the retention settings in BlinkClip's provider accounts; contact us if you want information about or deletion of data that can reasonably be associated with your browser.

Android beta signup and feedback

Android beta registration collects your verified Google Account email address, optional device information, and optional comments. We use this information to coordinate beta invitations, testing, feedback, and related follow-up. Feedback submissions include whatever issue descriptions and other details you choose to send. These submissions are separate from the Android app’s local media processing.

Registration responses are handled through Google Forms and Google Sheets. Feedback sent by email is handled by your email provider and ours. We use the information to manage the test and improve BlinkClip, and provide your Google Play email to Google Play to manage tester access. After the required Google Play approval and enrollment configuration are complete, self-service enrollment will use Google Groups and Google Play: Google will handle your membership, account details, and opt-in through those services, and group managers can access membership details for beta administration. Submitting the optional form is separate from any future group enrollment and Google Play opt-in. You can leave the group and opt out through Google’s services. We do not send form responses or tester email addresses to website analytics. Registration information is retained only as long as needed for beta administration and related follow-up. To request deletion or leave the tester list, email blinkclip.app@gmail.com.

9. Service providers and external services

  • GitHub Pages and its content-delivery providers host the website, and GitHub Releases distributes Mac updates and downloads. They receive standard request data such as IP address, time, requested URL, and browser or client information.
  • Google Analytics provides website measurement under the consent behavior described above.
  • Amplitude EU provides optional product analytics only after you allow analytics.
  • YouTube receives a request when you choose to load or open an embedded demo.
  • Your email provider and ours process messages you send to support, including your address and anything you include.
  • Apple, Google Play, browser, file, Photos, cloud-storage, and share providers may process data when you use their stores, pickers, destinations, or services. BlinkClip does not control their independent practices.

BlinkClip does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Provider access is limited to operating, distributing, supporting, and measuring BlinkClip as described in this policy.

10. Storage, retention, and deletion

  • Native apps: local preferences and BlinkClip-owned working copies remain until you remove or reset them, clear app data, or uninstall the app, depending on the platform. User-exported files are not removed by clearing BlinkClip data.
  • BlinkClip Online: working data is held in the tab's memory and virtual file system for the session and cleaned as described above. Downloaded files remain under your control.
  • Website choices: you can change analytics consent on this page and clear BlinkClip site data in your browser. Disabling analytics clears known GA and Amplitude browser cookies and stored analytics attribution on a best-effort basis.
  • Support: support messages remain in the relevant email systems for as long as needed to answer the request, maintain necessary business records, and handle follow-up, after which they may be deleted.
  • Hosting and analytics: providers retain request and analytics records under their account settings and policies. BlinkClip does not promise a fixed period that the reviewed source cannot verify.

BlinkClip has no user accounts, so there is no account-deletion flow. For data held by BlinkClip rather than only on your device, email blinkclip.app@gmail.com with enough information for us to locate the record. We may need to verify the request.

11. Your privacy choices and rights

You can choose which media to import, remove local BlinkClip copies and results, choose whether to save or share an export, disable optional analytics, clear site data, and stop using or uninstall a product. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or a portable copy of personal data we hold. You may withdraw consent for optional analytics at any time; this does not change earlier processing. Contact us to exercise a right. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

12. Security, international processing, and children

Security

BlinkClip limits media access through platform pickers and app or browser isolation, keeps native and online processing local where described, and serves the public website and update resources over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and this policy does not promise absolute security or encryption beyond what the reviewed platforms and connections provide.

International processing

Local media processing occurs on your device. Website hosting, analytics, downloads, updates, and email can be processed in countries other than the one where you live. Amplitude is configured to use its EU data zone; other providers may process data through their global infrastructure subject to their legal safeguards.

Children

BlinkClip is a general-purpose media utility and is not directed to children under 13 or the higher minimum age required where they live. The products do not knowingly create profiles of children. If you believe a child sent personal information to BlinkClip through support or another network feature, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

13. Changes to this policy and contact

We may update this policy when BlinkClip's products, providers, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top identifies the published version. Material changes will be posted on this page before or when they take effect where practical.

Questions or privacy requests: blinkclip.app@gmail.com.
