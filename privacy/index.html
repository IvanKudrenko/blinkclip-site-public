Local processing first.

BlinkClip is built around local macOS video processing. These notes explain what happens on your Mac and what happens when you contact support.

Privacy notes

Plain privacy details for early access.

Video processing

Core batch video processing happens locally on your Mac. Your clips do not need to be uploaded for the main cleanup flow.

Support email

When you email blinkclip.app@gmail.com, your message and any context you include are used to answer the request and improve BlinkClip.

External links

Downloads, release notes, and demo videos may open GitHub, YouTube, or your email app. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Website analytics

With your permission, BlinkClip uses Amplitude to understand site visits, returning anonymous browser or device activity, referral and campaign sources, approximate country or region, and DMG download interactions. Amplitude stores a randomly generated browser or device identifier in analytics cookies to recognize returning anonymous visitors. It does not identify you by name, and clearing cookies, using private browsing, or changing browser or device may create a new identifier. Your network IP address may be used to derive approximate country or region; BlinkClip does not request GPS or exact location. Analytics starts only after you allow it, uses Amplitude's EU data region, and does not use Session Replay or broad interaction capture.
