Use cases

One tool for the jobs creators repeat every week.

YouTube intros and outros

Batch brand a folder before uploading instead of re-opening the same project again.

Consistent channel packaging across every clip in the upload batch.

TikTok and Reels resizing

Prepare vertical exports from longer recordings without rebuilding the crop every time.

Faster social repurposing with cleaner framing passes.

Podcast or lecture cleanup

Remove slow openings, static pauses, and repeated ending sections from sessions.

Cleaner long-form material before publishing or sending to an editor.

Gaming highlight batches

Trim, stitch, and export recurring clips for a steady upload rhythm.

More clips ready for queueing without manual export repetition.

Client deliverables

Create consistent files across multiple versions without manual timeline repetition.

Polished deliverables with fewer missed export settings.

Archive conversion

Normalize folders into practical formats for storage, transfer, or reuse.

Organized libraries that are easier to move, store, and reuse.

Upload-ready delivery batches

Compress and resize a folder with consistent output settings before publishing or client review.

Smaller, correctly sized files ready for faster upload and handoff.

Audio cleanup and localization

Extract, mute, replace, or mix audio across related videos without rebuilding a timeline for each one.

Cleaner audio handoffs and faster preparation for alternate language or music tracks.

Branded overlays and inserts

Apply recurring logos, watermarks, sponsor media, or disclaimer clips with consistent timing.

Repeatable campaign packaging without manually positioning every asset.
